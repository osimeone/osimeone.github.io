Professor & IEEE Fellow · Northeastern University London

I work at the intersection of information theory, machine learning, and quantum information processing. My group develops principled methods for reliable and efficient communication, computing, and learning systems — from Bayesian learning and neuromorphic computing to quantum machine learning and conformal prediction.
